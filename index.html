About this animation

A drone-style flight over Norwegian fjords and coastline, followed by a tsunami. Land is flooded wherever the water surface rises above Kartverket's terrain height.

This is an illustrative visualisation, not a hazard or risk model. The wave shape, height and speed are stylised and time-lapsed, the sea floor is synthetic, and the city lights are randomly placed on low-lying land.
